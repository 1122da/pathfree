Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Path Free: Arrow Puzzle (“the App”) handles information. The App is a single-player offline puzzle game for Android (package name: com.pathfree.arrowpuzzle).

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information. It does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs in the current version.

Gameplay data is stored only on your device, including items such as:

  • Unlocked / current level progress
  • Coins and best star results per level
  • Settings (sound, music, vibration)
  • Selected visual theme

This information stays on your device unless you uninstall the App or clear app data.

2. Internet access

The App does not require an internet connection to play. The current version is designed to work offline.

3. Permissions

The App may use:

  • Vibration — only for optional haptic feedback during gameplay (if enabled in settings).

4. Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because it does not collect personal data online, there is no personal information for us to delete from servers.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user personal data with third parties, because the App does not collect personal data in the current version.

6. Changes to this policy

If the App’s data practices change in the future (for example, if ads, analytics, cloud save, or accounts are added), this Privacy Policy will be updated and the “Last updated” date will change.
